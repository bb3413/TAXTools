Social Security Tips

If the employee receives more than $20 per month in cash tips at one job, they are supposed to report the tip income to their employer. Cash tips include tips paid by cash, check, debit card, and credit card. The employer will show these reported tips in Boxes 1 and 7 on Form W-2. The amounts reported on the W-2 have already been subject to FICA taxes (Social Security and Medicare) so the taxpayer will not need to pay FICA taxes again on these amounts.

Allocated Tips

Allocated tips are amounts an employer assigned to the employe in addition to the tips they reported. Social Security and Medicare taxes are not withheld on allocated tips. Allocated tips are not included in the amount in Form W-2, Box 1.

To ensure that everyone reports their fair share of income from tips, some employers have tip allocation programs, which are approved by the IRS. If an employee reports tips to the employer that were less than the designated share based on the employer’s formula, the employer reports the difference as allocated tips and includes it on the employee’s Form W-2.

Dependent Care Benefits

This is used by employers to report dependent care benefits paid to the taxpayer or incurred on behalf of the taxpayer. This benefit is taxable unless Form 2441, Child and Dependent Care Expenses, is completed. If the taxpayer spends less than their employer-provided benefit, the balance is taxable.

Nonqualified Plans

This amount is included in box 1 if it is a distribution made from a non-qualified deferred compensation or nongovernmental section 457(b) plan, or included in box 3 and/or box 5 if it is a prior year deferral under a non-qualified or section 457(b) plan that became taxable for Social Security and Medicare taxes this year because there is no longer a substantial risk of forfeiture.

Codes

There are numerous code letters that the employer can use to designate certain items, such as employee contributions to the company retirement plan.

Checkboxes

Statutory employees receive Form W-2 for their pay and have taxes withheld; however, statutory employees may report their income and deduct their expenses on schedule C.

The retirement plan box indicates that the employee participated in the employer’s retirement plan. If the taxpayer or spouse (if MFJ) makes traditional IRA contributions, their deduction may be limited.

Third-party sick pay is reported on Form W-2 by the insurance company when it is taxable. If there is no amount in box 1, then none of the sick pay is taxable.

Information

This box may be used by employers for other information, such as union dues, uniform payments, health insurance premiums paid through payroll deduction, or non-taxable income. Mandatory contributions to state disability, should be deducted on schedule A as a state income tax.

Generally, none of the box 14a items are taxable for federal purposes. Many are informational only and do not impact the tax return.
